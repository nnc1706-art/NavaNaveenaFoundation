ABOUT US

Dedicated to Better Healthcare,
Education & Research

Nava Naveena Foundation is a public charitable trust committed to improving the lives of individuals through accessible healthcare, patient support, medical education, research, awareness programmes and community initiatives.

The foundation supports people affected by endocrine disorders and Type 1 Diabetes through medical assistance, educational programmes, scientific conferences, preventive healthcare, patient counselling and community awareness initiatives.

Learn More

Our Mission

To improve community health through accessible healthcare, medical education, scientific research, awareness programmes and compassionate patient support while ensuring quality care reaches everyone.

Our Vision

To build healthier communities by empowering individuals through research, education, preventive healthcare, innovation and long-term patient care.

OUR OBJECTIVES

Making a Positive Impact

Our foundation works towards improving healthcare, medical education, scientific research and community well-being through sustainable initiatives.

Health Development

Free and subsidized insulin, medicines, medical camps and emergency care for children and adults with Type 1 Diabetes and endocrine disorders.

Development Programmes

Free diagnostic screening, HbA1c and thyroid testing, and preventive health check-ups for early detection in under-served areas.

Education & Counselling

Workshops and one-on-one counselling for patients, parents and caregivers to improve understanding and self-management of chronic conditions.

School & Nutrition Support

Scholarships, educational materials and nutrition support so children with chronic endocrine disorders continue school without hardship.

Research & Data Initiatives

Patient registries, epidemiological data and scientific studies to improve treatment protocols and inform public health policy.

Training & Capacity Building

Workshops and certification courses for doctors, nurses, diabetes educators and community health workers.

Advocacy & Public Awareness

Awareness campaigns and policy engagement for early detection, disability inclusion and reducing stigma around endocrine disorders.

Partnerships & Outreach

Collaborating with hospitals, government bodies, NGOs and CSR partners to expand reach without discrimination of any kind.
