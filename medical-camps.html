OUR PROGRAM

Medical Camps

Nava Naveena Foundation organizes free medical camps to provide quality healthcare services for people who may have limited access to hospitals and healthcare facilities. These camps bring experienced doctors and healthcare professionals directly to the community.

Our medical camps focus on early disease detection, preventive healthcare, health education, and timely medical consultations. Through these initiatives, we aim to improve public health, create awareness, and encourage healthier lifestyles.

OUR SERVICES

Healthcare Services Offered at Our Medical Camps

Our medical camps provide essential healthcare services, preventive health screenings, medical consultations, and awareness programmes to improve community health and well-being.

General Health Checkups

Comprehensive health examinations help identify common health issues early and encourage timely medical care.

Health Screening

Free screening for blood pressure, diabetes, BMI, and other basic health parameters to promote preventive healthcare.

Doctor Consultations

Qualified doctors provide personalized consultations, diagnosis, treatment advice, and referrals whenever required.

Health Education

Interactive awareness sessions on nutrition, hygiene, disease prevention, and healthy lifestyle practices for all age groups.

WHY MEDICAL CAMPS

Bringing Quality Healthcare Closer to Every Community

Access to healthcare should never depend on where someone lives. Our Medical Camps are designed to reach underserved communities by providing free health services, medical advice, and preventive healthcare support.

Through these camps, we promote early diagnosis, improve health awareness, and encourage healthier lifestyles while connecting people with experienced healthcare professionals.

Free consultations with experienced doctors
Preventive health screenings
Community health awareness programmes
Improved access to quality healthcare
OUR PROCESS

How Our Medical Camps Work

Every medical camp is carefully planned to provide accessible, efficient, and quality healthcare services for every participant.

Registration

Visitors register at the camp and provide basic health information before medical consultation.

Health Screening

Basic health parameters such as blood pressure, blood sugar, weight, and BMI are checked.

Doctor Consultation

Experienced doctors examine patients, provide treatment advice, and recommend further care if required.

Health Education

Participants receive guidance on healthy living, nutrition, disease prevention, and follow-up care.
