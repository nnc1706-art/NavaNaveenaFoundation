Our Programs

Every Objective in Our Trust Deed, in Action

Our Trust Deed sets out eight areas of work — from direct medical assistance to research, training, and advocacy. Here's what each one means for the children, adults, and families we serve.

Support This Work Back to About
01 · CORE PROGRAM

Health Development

Our founding focus: making sure children and adults with Type 1 diabetes and other endocrine disorders get the treatment they need.

Medical Assistance

Free or subsidized insulin, essential medications, and medical supplies for children with Type 1 and Type 3c diabetes, prioritizing families from economically weaker sections and persons with disabilities.

Medical Camps

Health camps and medical care services for people in rural and urban areas, along with general health checkups across age groups.

First-Aid Education

Teaching children pre-hospital care for emergencies like injuries, seizures, dog bites, and burns, plus awareness of everyday health issues like fever and asthma.

Blood Donation & Safety

Blood donation camps, home and school safety awareness, and Yoga, meditation, and holistic wellness classes for the community.

02 · CORE PROGRAM

Diagnostic, Preventive & Medical Care

Early detection and consistent monitoring make the biggest difference in long-term outcomes for endocrine disorders.

Diagnostic & Preventive Care

Free or subsidized diagnostic services and health camps focused on endocrine health — community-based diabetic screening, HbA1c testing, thyroid and hormone level screenings, and preventive check-ups in under-served areas.

Medical Programs

Programs spanning medical and biomedical sciences, pharmacy, allied health, and nursing, aimed at delivering timely, quality healthcare backed by an efficient Hospital Management Information System.

03 · CORE PROGRAM

Education & Counselling

Supporting the knowledge, skills, and emotional wellbeing of patients, parents, caregivers, and families affected by endocrine disorders.

Knowledge & Skill Building

Workshops and educational materials that build understanding, practical skills, and self-management confidence.

One-on-One Counselling

Psychosocial support that reduces stigma, improves treatment adherence, and strengthens family wellbeing.

Personal & Career Guidance

Helping young people with self-understanding, decision-making, and preparation for further education and careers.

Adjustment & Adaptation

Support in coping with the changes, stress, and challenges that come with a chronic condition.

04 · CORE PROGRAM

School & Nutrition Support

Making sure a chronic endocrine condition never has to mean the end of a child's schooling.

Educational Continuity

Scholarships, financial assistance, and educational materials or assistive devices so children with chronic endocrine disorders can stay in school without interruption.

Nutritional Well-being

Nutritional supplements and diet-support programmes that protect a child's health and growth alongside their education, in an inclusive school environment.

05 · CORE PROGRAM

Research, Data & Training

Better data and better-trained caregivers lead to better outcomes for every patient we support.

Research & Data Initiatives

Patient registries, funded studies, and epidemiological data collection to improve treatment protocols and inform public health policy on endocrine disorders.

Training & Capacity Building

Workshops and certification courses for doctors, nurses, diabetes educators, nutritionists, and volunteers in endocrinology and diabetes care.

Community Health Workers

Building grassroots capacity so community health workers can provide awareness, basic support, and referrals at the local level.

Advocacy & Public Awareness

Awareness drives, media campaigns, and policy engagement to promote early diagnosis, disability inclusion, and reduce stigma around endocrine disorders.

GOVERNANCE

How We Put This Into Practice

Every programme above is carried out under the terms of our Trust Deed, which sets strict rules for how funds are used and reported.

No Personal Benefit

All income and property are applied solely to our charitable objects — no trustee, member, or donor profits personally.

Audited Annually

Accounts are maintained and audited every year by a Chartered Accountant, for the financial year 1st April to 31st March.

Open Partnerships

We collaborate with government health agencies, hospitals, educational institutions, other NGOs, and CSR partners to widen our reach.

Open to All

Every benefit and service is provided without discrimination on the basis of caste, creed, religion, gender, or social background.

Help Us Reach More Children and Families

Every rupee donated goes directly toward medical assistance, education, and support for those living with Type 1 diabetes and endocrine disorders.
