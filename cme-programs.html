OUR PROGRAM

Continuing Medical Education (CME)

Nava Naveena Foundation is committed to advancing healthcare by organizing Continuing Medical Education (CME) programs for doctors, specialists, researchers, and healthcare professionals. These programs provide opportunities to stay updated with the latest medical knowledge, clinical practices, and healthcare innovations.

Through expert-led lectures, interactive workshops, panel discussions, and scientific sessions, our CME initiatives encourage lifelong learning, knowledge sharing, and professional excellence to improve patient care.

OUR LEARNING PROCESS

How Our CME Programs Work

Our Continuing Medical Education programs are designed to provide structured learning experiences that help healthcare professionals enhance their knowledge and clinical expertise.

Registration

Healthcare professionals register for the CME session and choose topics based on their area of interest and specialization.

Expert Sessions

Experienced doctors and medical experts deliver lectures on the latest medical research, treatments, and clinical advancements.

Interactive Learning

Participants engage in workshops, case discussions, Q&A sessions, and collaborative learning activities.

Professional Growth

Participants gain updated medical knowledge, strengthen clinical skills, and continue their journey of lifelong professional learning.
